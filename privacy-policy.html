3. How we use your data

  • To provide, maintain and improve the Service.
  • To create your account and verify that you meet the 18+ age requirement.
  • To display your content and profile to other users as you direct.
  • To operate safety features: automatic content filtering, reporting, blocking, hiding and account suspension.
  • To review reports of objectionable content or abusive behaviour within 24 hours, remove violating content, and eject the accounts responsible.
  • To process in-app purchases and operate virtual currency.
  • To send service notifications you have enabled.
  • To detect, prevent and investigate fraud, abuse and security incidents.
  • To comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests from authorities.

Legal bases (where UK/EU GDPR applies)

  • Performance of a contract — operating your account and delivering the Service.
  • Legitimate interests — keeping the Service safe, preventing abuse and fraud, and improving the product.
  • Legal obligation — retaining records and reporting illegal content where required.
  • Consent — device permissions such as microphone, camera and notifications.

4. Moderation and safety data

When you report content or a user, we record the content identifier, the reason you selected, your user ID, the reported user's ID and the time of the report. We use this to review the report within 24 hours and to decide whether to remove content or suspend an account.

  • The user you report is not told who reported them.
  • When you block someone, we record the block so that it can be enforced on both sides. The person you block is not notified.
  • We keep records of removed content and terminated accounts so that banned users cannot simply return, and so that we can respond to legal requests. These records are retained for up to 24 months after the moderation decision.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with the following:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC) — authentication, Realtime Database, Cloud Storage, Cloud Messaging and Crashlytics. Firebase hosts the data that powers the Service. See Firebase privacy.
  • Agora — real-time voice transmission in audio rooms. Agora transmits live audio; we do not record room audio.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, App Store purchases and push notification delivery. Apple processes payment data; we never receive your full card details.
  • Other users — your profile, posts, reels, comments, room activity and anything else you choose to share is visible to other users according to the privacy setting you pick.
  • Law enforcement and regulators — where we are legally required to, or where we believe in good faith it is necessary to prevent serious harm, including child safety and credible threats of violence.
  • Professional advisers and successors — our auditors and lawyers, and any party to a merger or acquisition, subject to the same protections.

6. International transfers

Our service providers operate globally, so your data may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where data leaves the UK, EEA or another region with transfer restrictions, we rely on recognised safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account and profile data — while your account is active.
  • User content — until you delete it, or until your account is deleted. Deleted posts, reels and comments are removed from the Service immediately and purged from backups within 30 days.
  • Direct messages — until deleted by a participant or the account is closed.
  • Moderation records — up to 24 months after the decision (see Section 4).
  • Transaction records — as long as required by tax and accounting law, typically 7 years.
  • Diagnostic logs — up to 90 days.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to our processing of your personal data, and to receive a copy in a portable format.

  • Access and correction — most profile data can be edited in the app under Profile → Edit Profile.
  • Deleting your account — you can request deletion from within the app, or by emailing babble.app12@gmail.com. We will delete your account and associated personal data within 30 days, except for records we must keep for legal, accounting or safety reasons as described above.
  • Withdrawing permissions — manage microphone, camera and notification access in iOS Settings.
  • Complaints — you may complain to your local data protection authority. We would appreciate the chance to resolve it first.

To exercise any right, email babble.app12@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

9. Children

Babble is rated 18+ and is not intended for anyone under 18. We ask for your date of birth at sign up and refuse account creation to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child is using Babble, contact babble.app12@gmail.com and we will investigate and delete the account and its data promptly.

10. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit (TLS), access controls restricting staff access to what is necessary, authentication managed by Firebase Authentication, and server-side rules limiting what data each account can read or write. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you and the relevant authority of a qualifying breach as the law requires.
